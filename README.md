## 🚀 What is Check My WPM?

[Check My WPM](https://www.checkmywpm.com) is a free online tool to **measure your typing speed, practice the kind of typing you actually do, and have fun while you get faster.**

Most typing tests only use generic English words. Check My WPM goes further with dedicated tests for code, numbers, data entry, business writing, punctuation and more — so the score you get reflects real-world typing.

**Highlights**

- ⚡ **Live stats** — WPM and accuracy update on every keystroke

- 🎯 **Detailed results** — net WPM, raw WPM, accuracy, consistency, correct/incorrect characters and errors

- 🔒 **No account, no ads, no trackers** — your results stay in your browser
- 📱 **Works everywhere** — desktop, tablet and phone
- ♾️ **Unlimited and free**

## 🧮 How Scores Are Calculated

Check My WPM uses the **industry-standard definition of a "word": 5 characters**, including spaces and punctuation. This makes scores fair and comparable no matter how long or short the actual words are.

### Net WPM (your main score)

Net WPM counts **only correctly typed characters**:

```
Net WPM = (correct characters ÷ 5) ÷ minutes
```

**Example:** In a 60-second test you type 250 characters correctly.

```
(250 ÷ 5) ÷ 1 minute = 50 WPM
```

In a 30-second test, the same 250 correct characters would give `(250 ÷ 5) ÷ 0.5 = 100 WPM`.

### Raw WPM

Raw WPM counts **every character you typed**, right or wrong:

```
Raw WPM = (all typed characters ÷ 5) ÷ minutes
```

The gap between Raw WPM and Net WPM shows how much speed you are losing to mistakes. A small gap means you are typing cleanly.

### Accuracy

Accuracy is the **percentage of correct keystrokes**, and it **includes corrections** — a mistake still counts against accuracy even if you go back and fix it:

```
Accuracy (%) = (correct keystrokes ÷ total keystrokes) × 100
```

### Consistency

Consistency shows how **steady your speed was** throughout the test. A high consistency score means you kept an even pace instead of bursting and stalling.

### Summary

| Metric | What it counts | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Net WPM** | Correct characters only | Your real, usable typing speed |
| **Raw WPM** | All typed characters | Your hand speed before errors |
| **Accuracy** | Correct ÷ total keystrokes (incl. corrections) | How clean your typing is |
| **Consistency** | Variation of speed across the test | How steady your rhythm is |
| **Errors** | Incorrect characters | Where you need practice |

> 💡 **Tip:** Accuracy comes first. Aim for **95%+ accuracy** and speed will follow.

## 🏁 What Is a Good Typing Speed?

| WPM | Level |
|---|---|
| 0 – 25 | 🐢 Beginner |
| 25 – 40 | 🙂 Average |
| 40 – 60 | 👍 Above average |
| 60 – 80 | 🚀 Fast (professional level) |
| 80 – 100 | 🔥 Very fast |
| 100+ | 🏆 Elite |

The average typist types around **40 WPM**; **60 WPM** and above is considered fast.

**Job benchmarks (data entry):** most roles expect at least **40 WPM at 95% accuracy**, and many prefer **60 WPM at 98%** or higher. Numeric keypad roles typically look for **8,000–10,000 KPH** (keystrokes per hour).

## 💡 Tips to Type Faster

1. **Focus on accuracy first** — aim for 95%+ before pushing speed.
2. **Learn touch typing** — keep your fingers on the home row (`ASDF` / `JKL;`) and don't look at the keyboard. Try the [Home Row](https://www.checkmywpm.com/typing-test-home-row) test and [Typing Quest](https://www.checkmywpm.com/games-typing-quest).
3. **Practice a little every day** — about **15 minutes daily** beats a long session once a week.
4. **Practice what you actually type** — developers should use the Developer test, office workers the Business or Data Entry tests.
5. **Keep a steady rhythm** — watch your Consistency score; even pacing is faster than bursts.
6. **Mind your posture** — sit straight, wrists relaxed, screen at eye level.
